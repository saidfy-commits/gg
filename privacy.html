Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Horror Legends Uploader ("the App") accesses and uses information when connecting to your Google Account for the sole purpose of uploading videos to a YouTube channel.

What this App is

Horror Legends Uploader is a personal automation tool. It generates short narrated video content and uploads it to a single, specific YouTube channel owned by the developer. It is not a public-facing service, and it is not intended for use by the general public.

What data we access

The App requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope from the YouTube Data API v3. This scope allows the App to:

  • Upload video files to the authorized YouTube channel
  • Set basic video metadata (title, description, tags) at upload time

The App does not access, read, or store any other data from your Google Account — including emails, contacts, Drive files, or personal information beyond what is strictly required to authenticate and upload a video.

How we use and store data

OAuth credentials (access token and refresh token) are stored securely as encrypted secrets within a private automation environment (GitHub Actions Secrets) and are used exclusively to authenticate upload requests to the YouTube Data API. No video content, metadata, or account information is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party outside of Google's own APIs and the infrastructure required to run the automation (GitHub, and the hosting providers used to generate video content).

Data retention

Credentials remain stored only for as long as the App is actively in use. You can revoke the App's access at any time via your Google Account permissions page , which immediately invalidates any stored tokens.

Third-party sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data obtained through this integration to third parties, other than Google's own YouTube Data API which the data is sent to as part of the App's core function (uploading a video).

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer at the email address associated with this project's Google Cloud account.
