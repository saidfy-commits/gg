Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

DriftLoop ("we", "the app") is a personal automation project. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. What data we handle

The app stores a TikTok API access token (issued via TikTok's OAuth flow) in order to publish videos to a single, designated TikTok account that we own and control. This token is stored as an encrypted secret in our automation environment and is never shared with third parties.

2. What we don't do

We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to TikTok viewers or any user other than the single account owner operating this app. We do not use this app to access, read, or post on behalf of any other TikTok account.

3. Third-party services

The app calls third-party AI services (for generating images and audio) using text prompts only. No personal data is sent to these services.

4. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time as the project evolves.

5. Contact

For questions about this project, please reach out via the TikTok account associated with this app.
